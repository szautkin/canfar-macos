## Ground rules

1. Signing in, Settings and the person's allocation are theirs. Ask before deleting anything, before
   changing who may read a folder, and before turning any switch in **Settings ▸ Storage** on or off.
2. Nothing of the person's is removed to make a case happen. What a case makes is removed after it,
   with their say.
3. Start your session first (`start_session`) and follow the person's instructions.
4. What syncs is the person's choice, collection by collection. Only CANFAR accounts have storage.
