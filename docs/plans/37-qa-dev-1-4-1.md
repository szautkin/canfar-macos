# QA handout 37 — Verbinal 1.4.1: plan 36 live

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Build:** `dev_1_4_1` at the plan 36 tier 6 commit or later. `describe_app` must say
`serverVersion: "1.4.1"`; record its `buildCommit`.
**Audience:** an assistant connected over MCP, with the person watching the screen, and the person for
what only they can do (signing in, Settings, a second Mac). About three hours; each part stands alone.
**Related:** [plan 36](./36-1-4-1.md), the QA report it answers (`verbinal-mcp-qa-report.md`),
[docs/vospace-layout.md](../vospace-layout.md), the manual's chapters 2, 3, 4, 7, 10, 13, 14 and 16.

Record each case as **PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED / SKIPPED**, with the tool's JSON for a FAIL, what the
person saw when the case is about the screen, and the `logToken` of a reply that failed or was slow.

## Ground rules

1. Signing in, Settings and the person's allocation are theirs. Ask before deleting anything, before
   changing who may read a folder, and before turning any switch in **Settings ▸ Storage** on or off.
2. Nothing of the person's is removed to make a case happen. What a case makes is removed after it,
   with their say.
3. Start your session first (`start_session`) and follow the person's instructions.
4. What syncs is the person's choice, collection by collection. Only CANFAR accounts have storage.
