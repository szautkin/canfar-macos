**Date:** 2026-10-08

record its `buildCommit` (a `+` means uncommitted changes).
**Audience:** an assistant connected over MCP, with the person watching the screen, and the person for
the cases only they can do (signing in, Settings, a second Mac). About two hours. Each part stands alone.
**Related:** [plan 34](./34-dev-1-4-1.md), [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md),
[docs/vospace-layout.md](../vospace-layout.md), the manual's chapters 1, 2, 3, 9, 12, 14, 15 and 16.

Record each case as **PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED / SKIPPED**, with the tool's JSON for a FAIL, what the
person saw when the case is about the screen, and the `logToken` of a reply that failed or was slow.

## Ground rules

1. Signing in, Settings and the person's allocation are theirs. Ask before deleting anything, before
   changing who may read a folder, and before turning **Keep Research in VOSpace** on or off.
2. Nothing of the person's is removed to make a case happen; a test observation or file made for the
   case is removed after it, with their say.
3. Start your session first (`start_session`) and follow the person's instructions.
