# Verbinal user manual

Verbinal is a Mac app for astronomers who work with the Canadian Astronomy Data Centre (CADC) and the
CANFAR science platform. It searches the archive, keeps your observations and notes, shows FITS images,
spectra and data cubes, manages your VOSpace storage, and runs sessions, batch jobs and code on CANFAR,
all in one window. An AI assistant can work in it with you, on terms you set.

This manual covers **Verbinal 1.4 for macOS**, on macOS 14 or newer. [Version française](../fr/00-contents.md).

Verbinal also exists for [Windows](https://github.com/szautkin/CanfarDesktop) and for
[Linux](https://github.com/szautkin/CanfarDesktopUbuntu), as separate apps. Their manuals will come
later; until then, their repositories describe them.

## How to read this manual

- Words in **bold** are what you see on the screen: a button, a field, a tab. They are written as the app
  writes them.
- **Settings ▸ AI Agent** means: open Settings, then its AI Agent section.
- ⌘ is the Command key, ⇧ Shift, ⌥ Option, ⌃ Control, ↩ Return, ⌫ Delete.
- Numbers on a picture match the numbered list after it.
- Each chapter ends with what an AI assistant can do there, for when you work with one.
- The pictures show Verbinal in dark mode; in light mode the screens are the same.

Start with [Getting started](01-getting-started.md). If you only want to look at a FITS file, open the
[FITS Viewer](04-fits-viewer.md): no account is needed.
